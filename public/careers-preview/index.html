Working With Seraphis

Specialist capability for real enterprise delivery

Our opportunities are built around client outcomes and technical delivery. We look for people who can show what they personally delivered, the technologies they used and the results they helped achieve.

Project-based by design

Many opportunities are contract or project-based and may depend on client requirements or successful project awards. We state this clearly rather than presenting every role as permanent employment.

Certifications support evidence

Certifications matter where clients require them, but they are strongest when backed by referenceable implementations, support work and measurable delivery responsibility.

Clear consent and role ownership

Your CV may be held in our specialist resource pool, but Seraphis will seek appropriate permission before proposing you as a named delivery resource to a customer or bid.
